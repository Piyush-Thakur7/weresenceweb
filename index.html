How to Install on Your Phone

Takes less than 30 seconds. No complicated steps.

1

Download APK

Tap the download button. The APK file will save directly into your phone's downloads folder.

2

Tap to Install

Open the file. If Android asks, tap "Allow from this source" to finish installation.

3

Start Chatting

Log in with your number or Google account. Set your Master PIN and you're good to go.
